My Favourite Recipe - Maggi

I like Maggi because it is easy to make and tasty.

Ingredients

  • Maggi noodles
  • Water
  • Oil
  • Onion
  • Salt

Preparation and Cooking Time

Preparation time: 5 minutes

Cooking time: 5 minutes

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped onion.
  3. Add water to the pan.
  4. Add Maggi noodles and tastemaker.
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes and serve hot.
